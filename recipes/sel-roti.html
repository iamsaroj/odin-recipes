Sel Roti

Description

Sel roti is a traditional Nepali ring-shaped, slightly sweet rice bread, deep-fried until crisp outside and soft inside. It's a festival staple, especially during Dashain and Tihar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 ripe banana, mashed
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
  • Water as needed
  • Oil or ghee for deep frying

Steps

  1. Mix rice flour, sugar, mashed banana, milk, and cardamom into a smooth, pourable batter.
  2. Let the batter rest for 3–4 hours (or overnight) to ferment slightly.
  3. Heat oil in a deep pan.
  4. Pour the batter in a circular motion into the hot oil to form a ring shape.
  5. Fry until golden brown on both sides, then remove and drain excess oil.
  6. Cool slightly and serve.
