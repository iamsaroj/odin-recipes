Dal Bhat

Description

Dal bhat is Nepal's staple meal — steamed rice served with a lentil soup, usually alongside a vegetable curry and pickle. It's simple, filling, and eaten daily across the country.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup lentils (masoor or moong dal)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee

Steps

  1. Rinse rice and cook in a rice cooker or pot until tender.
  2. Rinse lentils and boil in water with turmeric until soft, about 20 minutes.
  3. Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds until they crackle, then add onion and garlic. Sauté until golden.
  4. Pour the onion-garlic mix into the cooked lentils, stir, and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and serve hot over the rice.
