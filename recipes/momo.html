Momo

Description

Momo are Nepal's favorite dumplings — a thin dough wrapper filled with spiced meat or vegetables, steamed, and served with a tangy tomato-based achar (dipping sauce).

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup water (for dough)
  • 250g minced chicken or buffalo meat
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 2 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Knead flour and water into a smooth dough. Cover and rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Mix meat, onion, garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, coriander, salt, and pepper into a filling.
  3. Roll dough into small thin circles.
  4. Place a spoonful of filling in each circle and pleat the edges to seal.
  5. Steam the momo for 15–20 minutes until the wrapper turns translucent.
  6. Serve hot with tomato achar.
